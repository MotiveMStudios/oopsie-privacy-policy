Oopsie Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026 · App: Oopsie (com.motivemstudios.oopsie) · Developer: MotiveM Studios

In short
  • Oopsie works completely offline. The app has no internet permission and cannot send your data anywhere.
  • Everything you enter is stored only on your device. MotiveM Studios never receives, sees, sells or shares it.
  • No accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your data leaves your device only if you choose to export it yourself.

1. Who we are

Oopsie is a period and ovulation tracking app published by MotiveM Studios ("we", "us"). This policy explains how Oopsie handles your information. Because Oopsie is designed to keep your data on your device, we do not collect personal data from you through the app.

2. Information stored on your device

To work, Oopsie stores the following information only on your device, in the app's private storage:

InformationPurpose
Period dates, flow, symptoms, moods, discharge, sexual activity, ovulation test results, basal body temperature and notes you choose to logShowing your history and predicting your cycle, fertile window and ovulation
Your name (optional)Greeting you on the home screen
App settings (cycle settings, theme, reminders)Personalising the app
App lock PIN and optional secret wipe codeProtecting the app. They are stored only as salted, one-way hashes, never in readable form
Whether Oopsie Premium is unlockedEnabling Premium features

This information never leaves your device through Oopsie. It is excluded from Android cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer, so it is not copied to Google or to another phone.

3. Information we collect

We do not collect, receive or store any personal or health information. Oopsie contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software, and it does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or advertising ID.

4. Purchases (Oopsie Premium)

Oopsie Premium is an optional one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google processes the payment and handles your payment details under the Google Privacy Policy. Oopsie only learns from Google Play whether Premium is unlocked on your account. Your health information is never shared with Google as part of a purchase.

5. Exporting your data

With Oopsie Premium you can export your logged data as a spreadsheet (CSV) and send it with an email app of your choice. This happens only when you ask for it and confirm. The email is sent by your email app, not by Oopsie, and once sent it is handled by your email provider and the recipient under their own privacy policies. Oopsie deletes its own temporary copy of the export once it is a day old, the next time you open the app.

6. Notifications and permissions

Oopsie asks only for the permissions it needs:

  • Notifications: optional reminders (upcoming period, late period, check-in). They are created by your device, and their details are hidden on your lock screen.
  • Run at startup: to restore your reminders after your phone restarts.
  • Biometrics: optional fingerprint or face unlock, handled by Android. Oopsie never receives your biometric data.
  • Google Play billing: to offer Oopsie Premium.

7. How long data is kept

Entries are deleted automatically from your device once they are older than 5 months, or 24 months with Oopsie Premium. Entries logged while Premium was active keep the 24-month period. Settings are kept until you delete them.

8. Deleting your data

You can delete your data at any time:

  • Uninstall Oopsie, or open your phone's Settings → Apps → Oopsie → Storage → Clear storage. This removes everything and is always free.
  • With Oopsie Premium, use Settings → Privacy → Delete all data, or enter your secret wipe code on the lock screen to erase everything instantly.
  • If you forget your PIN, choose "Forgot PIN?" on the lock screen to erase everything and start again.

Because we never hold a copy of your data, we cannot recover it for you once it is deleted.

9. Security

Your data stays in Oopsie's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot access. You can add a PIN lock with optional biometric unlock. Please keep your device protected with a screen lock as well.

10. Children

Oopsie is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone. Because Oopsie does not send data to us, no information from any user reaches MotiveM Studios.

11. Medical disclaimer

Oopsie's predictions are estimates. Oopsie is not a form of contraception and does not provide medical advice.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be described in the app's release notes.
